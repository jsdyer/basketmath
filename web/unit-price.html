How to read a unit price

Divide the price by the total quantity in the package. For example, at $4.50 for 750 g the cost is $0.60 per 100 g. A 500 g bag for $3.50 costs $0.70 per 100 g, so the 750 g bag saves $0.10 for every 100 g you use.

For rolls or boxes, use the total sheet count. Six rolls with 80 sheets each contain 480 sheets. Compare products with similar sheet sizes and ply.
