How the calculation treats promotions

Regular price: each retail pack costs the price you entered. A multipack is one retail pack; enter its inner-unit count and the contents of each inner unit.

X packs for a total: complete groups receive the bundle total. Leftover packs cost the regular price. Each when buying X+: every pack receives the qualifying per-pack price once the threshold is met.

Buy X, get Y free: complete groups contain X paid packs and Y free packs. Add the free packs to the cart; they count toward your target. Any incomplete group charges for up to X packs. A note shows extra free packs available at the same outlay.

Coupons: per-pack coupons apply to paid packs (all packs in a priced bundle, excluding free BOGO packs). A purchase coupon applies once to this alternative's purchase. Per-pack coupons stop at each pack's price; complete bundles cap their combined coupons at the bundle price. Unused coupon value cannot reduce other packs. These rules assume coupons can be combined with the selected offer. Limits, member pricing and mixed-product offers are not inferred.

Rounding and units: entered prices use exact cents; conversions and promotion math use exact fractions. Estimated tax is rounded half-up once on the option's total. Unit prices are displayed to four decimal places and ranked using unrounded values. Weight ounces are avoirdupois; they are not fluid ounces. Weight and volume cannot be converted without a density.

Extra quantity is not automatically waste. The comparison assumes you can use everything bought; consider shelf life, freezer space and whether you would buy it without the offer.
